Loading a tune replaces the loops and song (your instruments, tempo and saved grooves stay). The melody becomes the Voice line and its chord symbols become the bar chords; add drums and layers on top. The tune book is the Nottingham Music Database, over a thousand traditional jigs, reels, hornpipes, waltzes and more in the ABC version made freely available by the ABC Music project.
